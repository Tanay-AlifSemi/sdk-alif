# OSPI — 60 / 80 / 120 MHz (characterization, not product)

**Board:** `alif_b1_dk` / `ab1c1f4m51820ph0` / `rtss_he`  
**SW:** Zephyr `e4aeaddf268f` (same as 40 MHz quote)  
**Quote SCK stays 40 MHz.** This file is extra: clocks are real; payload is not consistent.

**Logs:** `2026-09-15_b1_he_ospi_turbo240_120mhz_16kb_fail.txt`  
`2026-09-15_b1_he_ospi_turbo240_matrix_40_60_120.txt`

Firmware sweep can print `Max SCK read/write PASS: 60 MHz` or `120 MHz` after a **16 KB** increment pattern. **Do not quote that as product max.** Size matrix is the limiter.

Octal DDR. Size matrix = Test1 `55 aa 66 99` + 2…16384 B × 3.

---

## Even BAUDR (why 80 @ 240 is 120)

SCK = SSI / even(BAUDR). Driver: `BAUDR = DTS clock-frequency / bus-speed`.

| SSI | Request | HW BAUDR | Actual SCK | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 160 | 40 | 4 | 40 | fair — see `ospi_40mhz_fair.md` |
| 160 | 80 | 2 | **80** | DTS default; **not** reliable |
| 160 | 60 | 2 | 80 (not 60) | SKIP |
| 240 | 40 | 6 | 40 | fair |
| 240 | 60 | 4 | **60** | turbo-only extra |
| 240 | 80 | 2 | **120** | skipped in sweep; do not call it 80 |
| 240 | 120 | 2 | **120** | extra; hang risk |

If turbo overlay leaves `clock-frequency=160` while ACLK=240: software thinks BAUDR=2 for 80 MHz request, actual SCK = **120 MHz**.

## Size matrix

| Mode | SCK | BAUDR | Tap | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Normal 160 | 40 MHz | 4 | 11 | **43/43 PASS** (quote) |
| Turbo 240 | 40 MHz | 6 | 11 | **43/43 PASS** (quote) |
| Turbo 240 | **60 MHz** | 4 | 11 | **21 PASS / 22 FAIL** |
| Normal 160 | **80 MHz** | 2 | 11 | **19 PASS / 24 FAIL** |
| Turbo 240 | **120 MHz** | 2 | 6–7 | **6 PASS / 3 FAIL** (of 14, size < 1024 B); **skip ≥1024 B** (`flash_read` hang risk) |

Sweep **16 KB** at 60 MHz PASSes with the same 1 271 691 read cycles as 40 MHz. Size matrix then **21/22 FAIL**. 16 KB seed `0xaa` PASSes at 60 MHz; 16 B / 256 B / 4 KB FAIL every repeat.

120 MHz: LA on P4_2. Tap 11 → 16 KB `err=60`. RXDS window **taps 6–7** only (0–3 err=1016, 8 closes). Tap 6 can PASS 16 KB bench + XiP ~20.9 Mbit/s; size matrix still 6/3. Tap 7 16 KB `err=1`.

---

## How FAIL looks

| Rate | Pattern |
|---|---|
| 60 MHz | Exactly **1 bit per 4 bytes** (`err = length/4`: 16→4, 512→128, …). Systematic DFS / 32-bit stride. |
| 80 / 120 MHz | 1-bit drops, often bit 0: `55→54`, `aa→a8`. Often byte **[1]** or **[513]** (2nd byte of a 512 B beat). |
| All high rates | Full-chip erase **0 errors** on `0xFF`. Same sector then **43/43 at 40 MHz**. Not worn flash. |

Default DTS **80 MHz + tap 11 + baud2-delay=1**: functional **Test 1 FAIL** (`aa→a8`). Test 3/4 can still glance PASS.

---

## 120 MHz hang (bus / RXDS wait)

Erase OK. Write OK. **`flash_read` 1024 B** never returns (RXDS wait). Reset required.

High RXDS taps (≈24+) hang the same way. Passing window at 120 MHz was taps **6–7** only (write still done at 40 MHz, read-only tap walk).

Logic analyzer (Saleae ~500 MS/s): **~120 MHz** on P4_2 at BAUDR=2. Clock is real. 100/125 MHz bins and 25% duty are sample-grid artifacts.

This is **not** the LPUART P2_0 RX ISR storm. OSPI CS is **P2_1**. Hang is OSPI RXDS / `flash_read` wait, debugger may or may not halt depending on AES/OSPI lock.

---

## Timed bench at high SCK (when 16 KB PASSes)

`flash_read` 49 152 B stays **1 271 691 cycles** → **49.5 Mbit/s @ 160** / **74.2 Mbit/s @ 240**. Raising SCK does **not** raise cmd-path Mbit/s.

XiP `memcpy` can move with SCK (~20.9 Mbit/s @ 120 when it PASSes). Functional XiP vs `flash_read` at offset 0 **failed** at default 80 MHz (33–38 words).
